About This App

A Modern, Private Note-Taking Space.

This app is built for people who value **simplicity, speed, and privacy**.

We believe your notes are personal. That’s why this app is designed so **only you can read your notes** — not us, not the server, not anyone else.

Privacy by Design

Your notes are protected using **end-to-end encryption**.

What this means in simple terms:

  • Notes are **encrypted on your device**.
  • Only encrypted (scrambled) data is stored in the cloud.
  • Your password is used to unlock your private encryption key.
  • The server **never sees your notes in readable form**.

Even if someone accessed the database, they would see only meaningless encrypted data.

How It Works (Simple)

  • When you create an account, a private encryption key is generated just for you.
  • This key is protected by your password.
  • Every note you write is encrypted before it’s saved.
  • When you log in, your password unlocks the key and your notes are decrypted **locally on your device**.

If you don’t have the password (or a recovery option), the notes cannot be read — by anyone.

What We Can and Can’t See

This is a **zero-knowledge system** — even the app creators don’t have access to your data.

We Can See:

  • Your email address (for login)
  • Encrypted note data (unreadable)

We Cannot See:

  • Your notes or titles
  • Your content
  • Your encryption keys
  • Your password

Designed for Focus

Beyond security, the app is built to feel calm and intuitive:

  • Clean, distraction-free interface.
  • Smooth animations inspired by modern OS design.
  • Works seamlessly across devices.
  • Fast and responsive, even with large notes.

The goal is simple: **think clearly, write freely, and trust your data is safe.**

Important Note About Security

Because of how encryption works:

  • If you forget your password and have no recovery option, **your notes cannot be restored**.
  • This is intentional — it’s the trade-off for true privacy.

We recommend keeping your password safe and enabling recovery options when available.

Our Philosophy

Privacy is not a feature. It’s a foundation.

This app is built with the idea that your data belongs to you — and only you.
