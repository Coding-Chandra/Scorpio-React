About Notoz

A private place for your notes.

Notoz is an independent project published by webg, a solo developer. It is built for writing and syncing notes while keeping your note content encrypted before it reaches the server.

I can't read your notes because I don't have the key needed to decrypt them. Notoz encrypts them in your browser before they are sent to the server.

How Your Notes Are Protected

In plain language

  • Your notes are encrypted in the browser with AES-256-GCM.
  • The server stores ciphertext, not readable note content.
  • Your personal master encryption key is protected by a key derived from your password.
  • After login, the key is held in your browser as a non-extractable CryptoKey for the session.

Your password is important: if you lose it, there is no server-side password reset that can recover your encrypted notes.

What Notoz Includes

  • Client-side, end-to-end encrypted notes.
  • Account login and encrypted note syncing.
  • Sanitization of content pasted or synced into the editor before it is rendered.
  • A client-side security monitor that can warn about suspicious activity in your browser session and offer a one-tap logout.
  • User-initiated account deletion with password re-confirmation, including removal of the account and its encrypted notes.
  • Logout that clears the locally held session key from your device.

A Note About Security

Notoz uses the protections described above, but no online service can promise that every risk is eliminated. The security monitor is a convenience and awareness feature, not a guarantee against account compromise.

Independent by Design

Notoz is maintained by one independent developer, not a company or dedicated support team. Support response times may therefore be limited.
