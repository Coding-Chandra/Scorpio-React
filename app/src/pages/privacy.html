Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our notes application (“the App”). We designed our system so that your private notes stay private to you, and we cannot access them.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We intentionally collect as little information as possible.

We Collect:

  • Email address (for login, account creation, and password resets)
  • Display name (optional)
  • Encrypted note data (we cannot read it)

We Do Not Collect:

  • Plain text notes
  • Your password
  • Your Master Encryption Key
  • Any content you write inside the app

2. End-to-End Encryption

Your notes are encrypted locally on your device before being sent to our servers.

This means:

  • We cannot read your notes.
  • We cannot decrypt your notes.
  • We cannot restore your notes if you lose your password.
  • Your Master Key stays on your device.

Even if someone accessed the database, your data would appear as unreadable encrypted text.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

We store:

  • Encrypted note content
  • Encrypted Master Key (locked with your password-derived key)
  • Basic account information (email, display name)

We do NOT store:

  • Plaintext note content
  • Decryption keys
  • Your password

Your decrypted Master Key exists only in your browser storage (LocalStorage) while you are logged in.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your information only to provide the service, such as:

  • Authenticating your account
  • Syncing encrypted notes
  • Basic app features

We do not sell, share, or analyze your notes.

5. Loss of Access

Because of our encryption design:

  • If you forget your password, we cannot recover your notes.
  • If you delete your browser storage, we cannot restore your key.
  • If you delete your account, your encrypted data may become permanently inaccessible.

You are solely responsible for maintaining access to your password and device.

6. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers such as:

  • Supabase (for authentication & encrypted data storage)

This provider may process your email address and encrypted notes on our behalf. They cannot access your decrypted notes.

  • Netlify (for hosting our site)

They doesn't have access to our Database, They got our app files

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but:

  • No online service is completely immune from risks.
  • You use the App at your own risk.
  • We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

This app is not intended for users under the age of 13.
